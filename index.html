About this simulator

This tool implements IEEE 754 binary single-precision (32-bit) floating point operations entirely client-side, in vanilla JavaScript. Pick a tool below.

Decimal → Binary32

Convert a decimal number to its IEEE 754 binary/hex representation (and back), including special cases like NaN, Infinity, and subnormals.

Rounding Methods

See chopping, round-up, round-down, and round-to-nearest ties-to-even applied to the same value side by side.

Rounded Arithmetic

Add or multiply two operands (decimal or hex) with a selectable rounding method and full step-by-step working.
